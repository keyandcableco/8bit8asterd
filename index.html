8ASTERD ■

A control surface for the 8-Bit 8asterd: nine voices of chiptune across three AY-3-8910 chips, with effects built out of what those chips do when you push them past their limits. Everything below edits the unit live over USB. Save to Unit writes the settings into its memory so it keeps them with no computer attached.
